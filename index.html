Tuner and calibration

No song plays here. Sing or hum: the screen shows the note, how many cents off it is, and the range of notes you've held. Press 🎯 Tune again, or play, to go back to the song.

Mic latency

Sing on the clicks (with headphones): after 4 count-in clicks, sing a short "ta" exactly on each of the next 12. This measures the whole delay between what you hear and what the app judges.

Loopback: hold the headphones against the mic, or play through speakers, and stay quiet. The mic hears 10 clicks directly; this measures the audio system alone.

Settings

Kept per microphone. Measure it in 🎯 Tune, or set it by ear: raise it if the trail lands to the right of notes you sang in time, lower it if it lands to the left.

Octaves always count as hits. Folding also draws the ball next to the note, so a low voice on a high part still shows where it sits.
